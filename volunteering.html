Hatha Yoga Programs

Volunteer to support these classical yogic offerings, exactly as they are taught at Isha.

Surya Kriya

A potent yogic practice of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health, wellness and complete inner wellbeing.

Angamardana

A fitness system rooted in yoga that offers everyone the opportunity to invigorate the body and reach peak physical and mental health.

Yogasanas

A set of classical yogasanas that enable a person to flower into their ultimate potential — a natural process toward health, joy and blissfulness.

Bhuta Shuddhi

A whole system of yoga meaning "purification of the elements" — establishing wellbeing by purifying the five elements within the human system.

Shanmukhi Mudra

A simple but subtle practice that brightens and rejuvenates the face and eyes, bringing about a state of balance and meditativeness.
